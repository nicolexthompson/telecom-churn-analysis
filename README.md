# Telecom Customer Churn Analysis

An Excel-based customer churn analysis examining **6,000+ telecom customer records** to identify churn patterns, high-risk customer segments, and potential retention priorities.

## Project Overview

Customer churn can significantly impact revenue and long-term customer value. This analysis uses Excel to explore customer demographics, geography, consumption patterns, competitor activity, and service offers to identify factors associated with customer churn.

**Objective:**
Identify key churn drivers and high-risk customer segments that could help inform customer retention strategies.

## Key Findings

* **26.86% overall churn rate** across 6,000+ customers.
* **Competitor devices and competitor offers** were each associated with approximately **37% of churned customers**, making competitor activity a major area of concern.
* Customers aged **69+ had churn rates exceeding 40%**, identifying an important high-risk demographic.
* Customer churn varied across **demographic, geographic, and consumption segments**, highlighting opportunities for more targeted retention efforts.

## Analysis Process

### 1. Data Preparation

Cleaned and structured the customer dataset to prepare it for analysis, including reviewing customer attributes and churn-related variables.

### 2. Exploratory Analysis

Segmented customers across variables such as:

* Age
* Geography
* Customer demographics
* Consumption behaviour
* Competitor activity
* Service offers

### 3. Churn Analysis

Calculated overall churn and compared churn rates across different customer segments to identify populations with elevated churn risk.

### 4. Dashboard

Built an interactive Excel dashboard using **KPIs, PivotTables, and PivotCharts** to communicate key findings and make high-risk segments easier to identify.

## Dashboard

**Key metrics include:**

* Total customers
* Churned customers
* Overall churn rate
* Churn by demographic
* Churn by geography
* Churn by consumption
* Competitor-related churn

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, retention efforts could prioritize:

1. **Competitor activity**
   Investigate why competitor devices and offers are strongly associated with churn and develop targeted competitive retention offers.

2. **Older customer segments**
   Customers aged 69+ show substantially higher churn and may benefit from targeted retention campaigns and customer experience initiatives.

3. **Segment-specific retention**
   Use demographic, geographic, and consumption patterns to develop more targeted interventions rather than applying a single retention strategy across the entire customer base.
